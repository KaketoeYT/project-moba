IDEA:
  Ballista is a living weapon where their main playstyle is creating a safe space for their teammates.
  They do so by carving paths through terrain or acting as a shield while the teammate on their back
  can do the shooting.

	part of The living weapons

Basic attack - (Left Mouse)

Slow rate - Medium Ranged
shots come from the big ballista on their back.

-----------------------

Ability 0 - Passive
Name

Become mountable - Toggle (R)
When an ally is on the back of you you are unable to cast Basic attack,
your teammate becomes untargetable and has less acurracy.

-----------------------

Ability 1 :
  ON CAST:
    You dash through terrain leaving it destroyed for a moment.
    Teammates can walk through the terrain during the duration.



Ability 2 - Active (E)
Name

You can shoot the teammate on your back.
The longer you charge the further they will go.



Ability 3 - Active (L Shift)
Name

Anchor yourself into the ground becoming CC immune and giving the teammate on your back
more accuracy for a short duration


-----------------------
Ability 4 - Active (Q) (ULT)
Name

Shoot a more powerful fast moving projectile in a straight line.
The projectile bounces off of walls. Projectile applies slow on hit.
