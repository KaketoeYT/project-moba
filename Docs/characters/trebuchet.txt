------------------------------------------------

IDEA:
	A zoning type mage, which focuses on long-range poking, artillery and area denial rather than hard bursts of damage.

DESIGN:
	Part of The Living Weapons.

	A siege-engine themed mage inspired by Xerath and Anivia.
	Trebuchet controls the battlefield through long-range bombardment,
	destructible structures and temporary terrain manipulation.

Ability 0 - Passive
	Name: Rangefinder

	Basic attacks that hit an enemy mark them for a short duration.

	When Trebuchet hits a marked enemy with Ability 1, the mark is consumed
	and the mortar impact creates a slightly larger shockwave.

	NUMBERS: (optional)
		Mark Duration:
		Bonus Shockwave Size:

---------------------------------

Ability 1
	Name: Catapult

	ON FIRST CAST:
		Pick up a stone and load it into the trebuchet, preparing to fire.

	ON SECOND CAST:
		Fire the trebuchet, launching the stone in a high arc like a mortar.

		On impact, the stone creates a small crater that cannot be walked over.

		The impact also releases a shockwave, pushing nearby entities away
		from the landing point.

	NUMBERS: (optional)
		Cooldown:
		Damage:
		Crater Duration:
		Shockwave Range:

---------------------------------

Ability 2
	Name: Fortification

	ON CAST:
		Place a destructible wall at the target location.

		The wall has its own health and can be destroyed after taking several hits.

		The wall blocks movement until destroyed.

	Passive:
		When a stone fired by Ability 1 destroys the wall, it collapses
		and leaves rubble on the opposite side of the impact.

		The rubble cannot be walked over and disappears after a short time.

	NUMBERS: (optional)
		Cooldown:
		Wall Health:
		Rubble Duration:

---------------------------------

Ability 3
	Name: Counterweight

	ON CAST:
		Release the counterweight of the trebuchet in a chosen direction.

		The counterweight travels a short distance, dealing minor damage
		and knocking enemies away.

		The recoil pushes Trebuchet in the opposite direction.

	NUMBERS: (optional)
		Cooldown:
		Damage:
		Knockback:

---------------------------------

Ability 4 (ULT)
	Name: Siege Protocol

	ON CAST:
		Select a large target area.

		Trebuchet begins loading and firing several massive stones
		into the designated area.

		Each stone lands after a short delay, dealing moderate damage
		and creating a small shockwave.

		The impacts leave temporary craters, restricting movement
		through the affected area.

		The final impact deals increased damage and knocks enemies away.

		Cooldown:
		Duration:
		Damage:
		Impact Count:

---------------------------------

SYNERGIES: (optional)
	Works well with:
		Characters who can group enemies together or force them
		into narrow spaces.

	Struggles against:
		Highly mobile characters who can quickly close the distance
		or dodge delayed artillery strikes.
