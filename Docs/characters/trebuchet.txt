Ability 1 :
	ON FIRST CAST: 
		you pickup a stone and ready your trebuchet

	ON SECOND CAST: 
		you fire the trebuchet arching upwards like a mortar,
		the stone lands and leaves a small crater (You cant walk over craters).
		and on impact it sends a small shockwave that pushes everything away from the impact area.


Ability 2:
	ON CAST:
		Place a wall, this wall has health and can be torn down after a few hits

	Passive:
		When a stone of Ability 1 last hits the wall, 
		the wall falls down and leaves rubble on the other side of where hit from.
		You are unable to walk over rubble, this rubble disappears after a bit.
